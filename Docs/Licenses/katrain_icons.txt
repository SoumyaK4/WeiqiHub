KaTrain-derived icons

Upstream project: https://github.com/sanderland/katrain

SWHub includes byte-for-byte copies of these files from KaTrain's `katrain/img`
directory:

AI-Settings.png, Alternative.png, Deeper all.png, Equalize.png, Extra.png,
Finish.png, General-Settings.png, Insert-Move.png, Load-Game.png, New-Game.png,
Save-Game-As.png, Save-Game.png, Sweep.png, Teaching-Settings.png,
Time-Settings.png, ai.png, analysis.png, dot.png, inner.png, local.png, play.png,
reset.png, and topmove.png.

KaTrain records the following Flaticon creator attributions for its icons:

- Equalize icon and Trash icon: derived from work by bqlqn from
  https://www.flaticon.com/
- Other menu icons, Finish, Collaboration, and Flag icons: derived from work by
  Freepik from https://www.flaticon.com/
- Collapse branch icon: derived from work by Kirill Kazachek from
  https://www.flaticon.com/
- Prune icon: derived from work by Pixelmeetup from
  https://www.flaticon.com/
- Reset icon: derived from work by Pixel Perfect from
  https://www.flaticon.com/
- Rotate icon: derived from work by Frey Wazza from
  https://www.flaticon.com/
