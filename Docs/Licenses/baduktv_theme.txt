BadukTV board theme

Upstream project:
https://github.com/upsided/Upsided-Sabaki-Themes

SWHub's BadukTV board texture is adapted from the upstream
`baduktv/goban_texture_smooth.png` asset. SWHub also includes byte-for-byte
copies of the following upstream BadukTV stone assets under different names:

- `glass_black2.png` as `baduktv_bb_0.png`
- `glass_black3.png` as `baduktv_bb_1.png`
- `glass_black4.png` as `baduktv_bb_2.png`
- `glass_white3.png` as `baduktv_ww_0.png`
- `glass_white.png` as `baduktv_ww_1.png`
- `glass_white2.png` as `baduktv_ww_2.png`
